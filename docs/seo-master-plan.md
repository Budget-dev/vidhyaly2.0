# Vidhyaly NEET SEO Master Plan: Total Search Domination (2026-2030)

## 1. Executive Summary & Site Architecture
The goal is to move from a generic educational site to India's most authoritative source for NEET UG preparation. We will use a **Hub-and-Spoke model** with 10,000+ programmatically generated pages.

### URL Structure (SEO-Friendly)
*   **Subjects:** `vidhyaly.com/neet-preparation/{subject}`
*   **Chapters:** `vidhyaly.com/neet-preparation/{subject}/{chapter-name}`
*   **Resources:** `vidhyaly.com/neet-resources/{type}/{topic}` (e.g., `/notes/human-physiology`)
*   **Tools:** `vidhyaly.com/tools/neet-{tool-name}` (e.g., `/rank-predictor`)
*   **Exam Updates:** `vidhyaly.com/neet-updates/{year}/{article-slug}`

## 7. NLP & Entity Optimization
Google looks for entities like:
*   *NTA (National Testing Agency)*
*   *NCERT (National Council of Educational Research and Training)*
*   *MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery)*
*   *AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences)*

**Action:** Ensure these entities appear in proximity to your target keywords in the first 100 words of every page.
